## Hello, I'm Tanmay!

I'm a passionate technology enthusiast with a deep interest in **cryptography**, **electronics**, **web development**, and **embedded systems**. My journey in tech has been driven by curiosity and a desire to build solutions that make a difference.

### What I Do

**Software Development**: I work with modern web technologies including React, Node.js, and Python to build scalable applications. I enjoy both frontend and backend development, with a focus on creating intuitive user experiences.

**Cryptography & Security**: I have a strong interest in data encryption and network security. Through my DENS blog, I explore various cryptographic algorithms, from classical ciphers to modern encryption techniques.

**Electronics & IoT**: From participating in the World Robotics Olympiad to working with Arduino and Raspberry Pi, I love building hardware projects that solve real-world problems. Circuit design and embedded systems are where software meets the physical world.

**Open Source**: I believe in the power of open-source software and contribute to projects that interest me. Collaboration and knowledge sharing are at the heart of innovation.
